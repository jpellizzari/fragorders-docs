# v1 Subscriber Migration

Frag Orders v2 is a new application with its own billing. If you subscribed to
the original Frag Orders (v1), your Premium access carries over automatically —
you keep it for free through the end of your current v1 billing period, with the
same price to continue in v2.

## What you'll see

When you log into v2 as a v1 subscriber:

- You have **Premium access right away** — nothing to buy.
- A notice on your home page explains the move and shows the date your
  carried-over access runs **through**.
- The notice has two buttons:
  - **Re-subscribe** — start your v2 subscription (same price as v1).
  - **Dismiss** — hide the notice. It stays hidden on every device you use.

## What you need to do

To keep Premium after your carried-over window ends, **re-subscribe in v2** before
that date. It's the same price you paid in v1.

If you don't re-subscribe, your access simply returns to the free tier when the
window ends — nothing is charged.
